# About Me

I'm a guitarist who lives and plays in Detroit. I love blending electric guitar with electronica, incorporating automated live looping over backing tracks performed by me. My biggest influences are Dream Theater, Joe Satriani, and Orbital.

## My Electronics Projects

I love building stuff with microcontrollers, especially Midi controllers. Check out my blog for detailed write-ups!
