# About Me

I'm a guitarist who lives and plays in Detroit. I love blending electric guitar with electronica, incorporating automated live looping over backing tracks performed by me. My biggest influences are Dream Theater, Joe Satriani, and Orbital. I like to call my genre of music Progtronica but I'm not sure how much my music lives up to that descriptor.

## My Electronics Projects

I love building stuff with microcontrollers, especially Midi controllers. I have many old projects I intend to write [blog posts](/blog) for and will be of course writing up new projects as they come along. My website is still quite new so check back or subscribe to my [RSS feed](/feed.xml) to stay update!
